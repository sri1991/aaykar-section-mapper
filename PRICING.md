# AaykarSetu — Pricing Strategy

> Launch pricing · April 2026

---

## Philosophy

Start small. One paid tier, three billing options. No complexity until V2 features justify it.

---

## Tiers

| | **Free** | **Pro** |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Individual exploring | Practising CA / consultant / finance team |
| **Section & Forms Mapper** | ✓ Unlimited | ✓ Unlimited |
| **AaykarMitra (AI chat)** | 10 queries/day | Unlimited |
| **Document Scanner** | ✗ | ✓ Unlimited |
| **DOCX Export** | ✗ | ✓ |
| **Regime Comparison Calculator** *(when built)* | ✗ | ✓ |
| **Section Bookmarks** *(when built)* | ✗ | ✓ |

---

## Billing Options

| Billing | Price | Effective/month | Saving | Best message |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Monthly** | ₹499 | ₹499 | — | — |
| **Quarterly** | ₹1,299 | ₹433 | ~13% | *"Save ₹200"* |
| **Half-yearly** | ₹2,399 | ₹400 | ~20% | *"2 months free"* |

> Half-yearly over annual — the 1961→2025 transition is time-boxed. CAs will budget for "this filing season + next", not a full year upfront.

---

## What to Watch After Launch

- **Low conversion** → free tier may be too generous. Tighten Scanner to 3 scans/month on free.
- **High conversion** → room to raise to ₹699–799 when V2 features (Bulk Scanner, CA-branded PDF) ship.

---

## Future Tiers (V2+)

Introduce **Team (2–5 seats)** and **Firm (up to 25 seats)** only after Bulk Scanner and CA-branded Client Report PDF are live. Don't sell what isn't built.
