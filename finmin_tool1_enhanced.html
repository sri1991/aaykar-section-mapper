Heads up — regime choice timing. Telling your employer which regime to use is provisional and only affects TDS. Your final regime choice is locked when you file your ITR (typically Jun-Jul 2027). Salaried taxpayers without business income can switch regimes year-on-year; business/professional taxpayers face lock-in restrictions (Section 202, IT Act 2025).

Indicative estimate. Rental income, business/professional income and full perquisite valuation rules are not yet modelled.
