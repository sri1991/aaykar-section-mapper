CLAUSE 1 — DESIGNATION AND COMMENCEMENT

The Employee shall be employed as Principal Consultant – Tax Advisory with
effect from 1st April 2024. This Agreement governs the terms of employment
for the Previous Year 2024-25 (Assessment Year 2025-26) and shall be
renewed annually.

CLAUSE 2 — COMPENSATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

2.1  The Employee shall receive a fixed monthly remuneration of ₹1,25,000
     (Rupees One Lakh Twenty Five Thousand) as salary chargeable under the
     head "Salaries" within the meaning of Section 15 of the Income Tax Act,
     1961.

2.2  In addition to the fixed salary, the Employee shall be entitled to
     performance incentives, the nature of which shall be determined solely
     by the Employer. Such incentives, if paid, shall constitute "profits in
     lieu of salary" as defined under Section 17(3) of the Income Tax Act,
     1961.

2.3  The Employer shall deduct tax at source on salary income as required
     under Section 192 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, based on the projected
     annual income and the tax regime chosen by the Employee.

2.4  The Employee may opt for the new concessional tax regime under
     Section 115BAC of the Income Tax Act, 1961, subject to declaration
     being submitted to the Employer before 1st April of each Financial Year.
     If no declaration is received, the Employer shall apply the default
     regime as per CBDT circular for the relevant Assessment Year.

CLAUSE 3 — PERQUISITES AND ALLOWANCES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

3.1  House Rent Allowance: The Employee shall receive HRA of ₹30,000 per
     month. The exemption shall be computed u/s 10(13A) of the Income Tax
     Act, 1961, subject to submission of monthly rent receipts.

3.2  Leave Travel Concession: LTC is granted as per the terms of Section
     10(5) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, for travel within India. The Employee
     is entitled to claim this concession twice in a block of four years.

3.3  Gratuity: Upon completion of five years of continuous service, the
     Employee shall be eligible for gratuity, which shall be exempt from
     tax as per Section 10(10) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, up to the
     prescribed ceiling under the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.

3.4  Leave Encashment: Any leave encashed at the time of retirement or
     separation shall be exempt u/s 10(10AA) of the Income Tax Act, 1961,
     subject to applicable limits.

CLAUSE 4 — PROFESSIONAL TAX AND DEDUCTIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

4.1  The Employer shall deduct Professional Tax as applicable under state law.

4.2  The Employee acknowledges that the standard deduction available under
     Section 16(i) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, shall be applied in computing
     taxable salary income. The deduction for entertainment allowance under
     Section 16(ii) and employment tax under Section 16(iii) shall also be
     applied as eligible.

4.3  The Employee may furnish proofs for deductions under Chapter VI-A
     (Sections 80C through 80U) of the Income Tax Act, 1961, to the Employer
     for purposes of TDS computation.
